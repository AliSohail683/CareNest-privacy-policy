CareNest

One shared place for everyone caring for someone they love — medications, appointments, and daily updates.

Caring for someone is rarely a one-person job

But most families run it out of a group chat. Who picked up the prescription? Was the evening dose taken? What did the cardiologist actually say? CareNest gives everyone caring for them one private space for all of it.

Medications that stay current

Snap a photo of a pill bottle or a full prescription and CareNest reads the name, dose and schedule. Everyone sees what was taken — and what was missed.

Share the load

A shared task board where pharmacy runs and rides to appointments get claimed, not forgotten.

Understand every visit

Visit notes turned into plain English the whole family understands, with suggested follow-up questions.

Everything in one place

Conditions, allergies, the care team, and an emergency card you can show any doctor or EMT.

Private by design. Your circle's information is visible only to the people you invite. CareNest never reads or stores your email address — you sign in with Apple or Google. We don't sell data and we don't use it for advertising.

Getting started

New accounts get 3 days free — no card needed. After that a subscription keeps the whole circle going: one subscription covers every member, so the rest of your family joins free.
