Common questions

How do I invite my family?

Open More → Care circle & invites and share your invite code. They install CareNest, create an account, choose Join with a code, and enter it — then you both see the same medications, tasks, and updates.

Does everyone need to pay?

No. One CareNest Premium subscription covers the whole circle — everyone else joins free.

The medication scan didn't work

Take the photo straight-on in good light, close enough that the label text is sharp. CareNest never guesses: if it can't read the label it tells you and offers manual entry rather than filling in a medication you didn't scan. Always check the extracted details against the label before saving.

I'm not getting dose reminders

Check More → Dose reminders is on, and that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → CareNest. Reminders are scheduled from your medications' dose times.

Managing or cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases on the CareNest paywall if a subscription isn't showing on a new device.

Deleting your account

More → Delete account, or see Delete your account.
